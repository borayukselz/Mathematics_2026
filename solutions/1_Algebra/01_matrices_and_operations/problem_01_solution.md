**1. State the sizes of matrices $A$ and $B$.**
*   **Explanation:** The size (or dimension) of a matrix is always described as "rows $\times$ columns". You count the horizontal rows first, then the vertical columns.
*   **Matrix $A$:** Has 2 horizontal rows and 3 vertical columns. Therefore, its size is **$2 \times 3$**.
*   **Matrix $B$:** Has 3 horizontal rows and 2 vertical columns. Therefore, its size is **$3 \times 2$**.

**2. Read off the entries $a_{12}$, $a_{23}$, $b_{21}$, and $b_{32}$.**
*   **Explanation:** In matrix notation, an entry labeled $x_{ij}$ refers to the specific number found in row $i$ and column $j$ of matrix $X$. The first number is always the row, and the second is the column. 
*   **$a_{12}$:** Look at matrix $A$, row 1, column 2. The entry is **$-1$**.
*   **$a_{23}$:** Look at matrix $A$, row 2, column 3. The entry is **$5$**.
*   **$b_{21}$:** Look at matrix $B$, row 2, column 1. The entry is **$-2$**.
*   **$b_{32}$:** Look at matrix $B$, row 3, column 2. The entry is **$1$**.

**3. Write the second row of $A$ and the first column of $B$ as vectors.**
*   **Explanation:** A vector is essentially a matrix with just one row (a row vector) or just one column (a column vector). You simply extract the requested line of numbers and keep them in their original orientation.
*   **Second row of $A$:** The numbers in the bottom row of $A$ are $0, 4,$ and $5$. Written as a row vector, this is **$\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 4 & 5 \end{pmatrix}$**.
*   **First column of $B$:** The numbers in the left column of $B$ are $1, -2,$ and $4$. Written as a column vector, this is **$\begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}$**.
